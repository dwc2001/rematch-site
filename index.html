Questions.

Do I need an account?

No. Open it and lift. There is nothing to sign up for.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. It stays on your iPhone, and you can export it any time.

How does it know when to add weight?

Stay in your rep range, 6 to 10 to start. When your first set hits 11, it suggests the lift's next step up. Every number is yours to change.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

It reads the heart rate your watch saves to Apple Health, and saves each workout there as strength training. There is no watch app yet.

When can I get it?

Rematch is in testing on iPhone now. It is coming to the App Store.
